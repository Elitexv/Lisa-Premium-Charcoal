How We Work

From Inquiry to Container Loading at Lagos Port

A clear, predictable process so you know exactly what happens at every stage of your order.

01

Inquiry & Sample Request

Send us your product, target quantity, destination port and any specification requirements.

02

Product Sampling

We arrange a sample shipment for your evaluation before you commit to a bulk order.

03

Quotation & Negotiation

We provide FOB/CFR/CIF pricing, lead time and payment terms tailored to your order.

04

Order Confirmation & Deposit

Order is confirmed with agreed payment terms (T/T or L/C) before production begins.

05

Production & Quality Control

Charcoal is processed, graded and checked for moisture, ash content and size consistency.

06

Packaging & Labeling

Product is packaged to your specification — standard bags, cartons or custom private-label.

07

Container Loading (Lagos)

Container is loaded and sealed at Apapa or Tin-Can Island port under supervision.

08

Shipping & Documentation

Full export documents are prepared and shared once the vessel departs.

Logistics

Shipping & Documentation Details

Port of Loading

Apapa Port or Tin-Can Island Port, Lagos, Nigeria — 20ft and 40ft containers supported.

Incoterms Supported

FOB (Free on Board), CFR (Cost & Freight), and CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight) — quoted per your request.

Documents Provided

Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary/Fumigation Certificate (where required).

FAQ

Common Questions from Buyers

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for charcoal export?
Our standard MOQ is one 20ft container. We can discuss smaller trial or sample orders for new buyers on request.
Which Incoterms do you support?
We quote FOB, CFR and CIF from Lagos port. Let us know your preferred term and destination port when you request a quote.
What documents are provided with each shipment?
We provide a Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, and Phytosanitary or Fumigation Certificate where required by the destination country.
Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?
Yes. We can arrange product samples for evaluation before you confirm a full container order.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) and Letter of Credit (L/C). Payment terms are confirmed at the quotation stage.
How long does shipping take from Lagos?
Transit time depends on your destination port and shipping line. We'll share an estimated timeline with your quotation once we know your destination.

Ready to Start Your Order?

Send us your product, quantity and destination port and we'll respond with a quotation and timeline.

About Lisa Premium Charcoal

Sourcing and Exporting Nigerian Charcoal with Integrity

We are a Lagos, Nigeria based export company dedicated to supplying international buyers with consistent, export-grade charcoal — from first inquiry to final delivery.

Our Story

Built to Connect Nigerian Charcoal with Global Buyers

Lisa Premium Charcoal was founded to give international buyers a direct, dependable route to premium Nigerian charcoal — without the inconsistency and communication gaps that make cross-border sourcing difficult. Nigeria's abundant hardwood species make it one of the best sources for dense, long-burning, low-smoke charcoal, and we exist to bring that quality to your market reliably.

From our base in Lagos, we manage sourcing, processing, quality checks and export documentation in-house, giving buyers a single point of contact for the entire order — not a chain of middlemen.

Why Nigerian Charcoal

Dense Hardwood, Long Burn Time, Low Smoke

Nigeria's tropical hardwood species — including Iroko, Azobe and other dense hardwoods — produce charcoal with excellent heat output, long burn duration and low smoke, making it highly sought after by BBQ brands, restaurants and shisha lounges worldwide. Combined with competitive FOB pricing from Lagos port, it's a strong sourcing option for importers looking to diversify supply.
